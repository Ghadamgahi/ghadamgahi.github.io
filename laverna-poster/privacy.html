Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

What data the tool uses

When the operator connects LAVERNA's own TikTok or Instagram account, the tool receives an access token and basic account details (account ID and username) from the platform. These are used only to upload videos to that same account.

Where it is stored

Tokens are kept in a local configuration file on the operator's own computer. They are sent only to TikTok's and Meta's official APIs. They are never sold, shared or sent to any other server.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect, store or process data about any other TikTok or Instagram users. This website has no analytics or tracking.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by revoking the app in TikTok or Instagram settings and deleting the local configuration file.
